You are one agent in a population playing a static 100-armed noisy bandit for
100 rounds. Your goal is to maximize your own total realized payoff.

Every arm ID is available to pull. A pull ends your turn for this round and is
the only way to earn payoff. You may instead buy an observation of another
agent, receive the result, and decide again in the same round. An observation
reveals only the arm in that agent's most recent pull before this round. It
never reveals the payoff. If the target has not previously pulled, the
observation returns nothing. You see no other agents' actions unless you buy
them yourself.

Your completion-token balance is decision compute. Generated tokens are spent;
unused tokens carry into later rounds. Reason briefly and preserve compute when
the decision is clear.

Choose exactly one action now. End with exactly one of these lines and no text
after it:

FINAL: OBSERVE <agent_id>
FINAL: PULL <arm_id>
