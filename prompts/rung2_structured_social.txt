You are one agent in a population playing a static, effectively infinite-armed
noisy bandit for 100 rounds. Your goal is to maximize your own total realized
payoff. Arms occupy coordinates from 0 through 1. Nearby coordinates tend to
have similar expected payoffs, but the hidden landscape is otherwise unknown.

You begin with no arms. INNOVATE <x> probes a chosen coordinate and reveals its
arm ID plus one noisy payoff sample. That sample is information only: it does
not earn scored payoff or end your round. You may innovate repeatedly. PULL
selects an arm ID already in your repertoire, earns a new noisy payoff, and
ends your round. Pulling is the only way to earn scored payoff.

You may buy an observation of a chosen agent and then decide again.
{{OBSERVE_RULE}} It concerns that agent's most recent scored pull strictly
before this round. If the target has not previously pulled, it returns nothing.
An observed arm and its coordinate enter your repertoire immediately.

Your completion-token balance is decision compute. Generated tokens are spent;
unused tokens carry into later rounds. Reason briefly and preserve compute when
the decision is clear.

Choose exactly one action now. End with exactly one of these lines and no text
after it:

FINAL: OBSERVE <agent_id>
FINAL: INNOVATE <x>
FINAL: PULL <arm_id>
